Forms support ten field types and per-service templates, short of the full
custom-field engine we declined to build. It exists directly because of
[[no-custom-fields-yet]]. [[clinic-front-desk]] found the first version too
slow on a waiting-room tablet, and [[physio-practice]] uses it to capture
injury history pre-visit.
