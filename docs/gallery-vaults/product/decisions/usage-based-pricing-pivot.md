[[tutoring-agency]] books 1,200 appointments a month on a 5-seat plan, which
flat pricing badly undercharged. We were also losing head-to-head evaluations,
per [[competitor-teardown-calendly]]. The pivot needs [[reporting-dashboard]]
usage exports to bill correctly, and [[pricing-experiment-results]] would
confirm the elasticity if it ever ran.
