In March 2025 we merged Starter/Pro/Team into a single page with add-ons.
[[photographer-solo]] priced us against Calendly for two weeks before signing,
per [[competitor-teardown-calendly]]. Simplifying cut sales-call pricing questions
from 40% to 12%, and the momentum fed directly into [[usage-based-pricing-pivot]].
