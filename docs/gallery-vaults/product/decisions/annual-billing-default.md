[[gym-regional-manager]] flagged the annual commitment as a churn risk during a
renewal call. We shipped [[deposit-payments]] the same sprint to soften the
upfront ask for smaller accounts. [[pricing-experiment-results]] should tell us
whether the toggle hurt conversion, but the experiment never shipped.
