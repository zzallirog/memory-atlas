Sales-assisted accounts appeared to churn at twice the self-serve rate, though
[[churn-cohort-analysis]] was never formally run to confirm it. [[spa-owner-churned]]
was the clearest case: a demo call closed a customer who cancelled in month two.
We paired the pause with [[founder-led-sales-pause]] to keep the funnel honest.
