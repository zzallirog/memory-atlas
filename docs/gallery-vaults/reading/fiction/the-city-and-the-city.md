description: China Miéville — two cities share one map, kept apart only by trained unseeing.
tags: [novel]
---
Citizens police a border that is pure discipline, no wall required — the
clearest fictional model of [[boundary-work]] I have found. The unseeing is
itself a made-up grammar, which nods at
[[invented-languages-as-worldbuilding]].
