Nabokov remembers himself with the same unreliable precision he gave his
narrators — a case that all memoir is [[unreliable-narrators|unreliable]] by
design. The recurring motifs are their own [[memory-palaces|memory palace]],
and a cousin of [[multiple-selves]].
