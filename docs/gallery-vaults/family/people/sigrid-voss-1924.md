Sigrid Voss, younger daughter of [[oskar-voss-1890]] and sister to [[ruth-voss-1921]], trained as a nurse at the county hospital through the 1940s. Her name sits beside her father's on the [[farm-auction-notice-1931]], the sale that eventually became [[the-farm-that-vanished]] in family memory. She kept a scrapbook of clippings long after the land itself was gone.
