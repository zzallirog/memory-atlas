Dora Voss, daughter of [[arvid-voss-1884]], sewed for Cedar Grove's finer households through the Depression and stood in the [[wedding-photo-1936]] beside her brother — the last clear image of her. She simply stopped appearing in any ledger or letter after 1940, a silence at the center of [[dora-after-1940]]. Cousins have guessed Minneapolis, but no address ever surfaced.
