Peter Voss, son of [[ruth-voss-1921]] and nephew to [[sigrid-voss-1924]], trained as a mechanic before trying, unsuccessfully, to buy back a piece of the old Voss acreage in the 1980s. The land itself was long gone by then, swallowed into the mystery of [[the-farm-that-vanished]]. He settled for farming a smaller plot two miles down the county road instead.
