Karl Lindqvist, older brother of [[ilse-lindqvist-1893]], built barns and porches across Cedar Grove as the town's go-to carpenter. Like his sister he is undocumented before 1899, part of the puzzle in [[the-gap-1893-to-1899]]. He is named as [[greta-voss-1887]]'s husband in a single ledger and stands as one of three petitioners on the [[naturalization-petition-1913]].
