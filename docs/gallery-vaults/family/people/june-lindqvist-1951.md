June Lindqvist, daughter of [[ernst-lindqvist-1918]], became the family's reluctant record-keeper after inheriting a box of her grandmother's papers. She grew up on stories of [[magdalena-voss-1861]] crossing the Baltic and of the [[letters-from-greta-1907-1919]] her great-grandmother Greta had once written. She now works as a small-town librarian, which her cousins consider fitting.
