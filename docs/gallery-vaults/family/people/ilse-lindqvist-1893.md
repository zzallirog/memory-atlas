Ilse Lindqvist taught the youngest grades at the Cedar Grove schoolhouse after marrying [[arvid-voss-1884]] at seventeen. She and her brother [[karl-lindqvist-1889]] show up nowhere in the family's papers before 1899, a silence chased in [[the-gap-1893-to-1899]]. The pair finally surface together, married and settled, on the [[census-sheet-1910]].
