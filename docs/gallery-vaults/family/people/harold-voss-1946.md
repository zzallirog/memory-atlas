Harold Voss, son of [[walter-voss-1912]], grew up on the same ground his great-great-grandmother [[magdalena-voss-1861]] had claimed decades before, a fact his mother reminded him of constantly. He kept the [[wedding-photo-1936]] of his parents framed above the farmhouse stairs his whole life. His younger sister [[anna-voss-1949]] used to tease him for dusting it weekly.
