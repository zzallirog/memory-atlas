Ernst Lindqvist, son of [[greta-voss-1887]] and reputedly [[karl-lindqvist-1889]], ran the auto shop on Cedar Grove's main street from the 1940s onward. He grew up hearing about his great-grandmother [[magdalena-voss-1861]] as the woman who started it all, and the [[letters-from-greta-1907-1919]] his mother kept span both sides of his birth. He rarely spoke of his own father, which the family took as its own quiet answer.
