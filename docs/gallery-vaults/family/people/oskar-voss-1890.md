Oskar Voss was thirteen when he crossed with [[magdalena-voss-1861]] and the rest of the family, his name entered on the [[steamship-manifest-1903]] and, if the disputed [[ellis-island-manifest-1903]] is believed, entered twice. He apprenticed as a blacksmith in Cedar Grove and shod half the town's horses by 1920. A cousin sharing his exact name has muddied county rolls ever since, the subject of [[two-oskars-problem]].
