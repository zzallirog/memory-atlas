Arvid Voss, eldest child of [[magdalena-voss-1861]], was baptized in Riga per the [[baptism-book-riga-1884]]. He married [[ilse-lindqvist-1893]] soon after the family settled, and by 1905 his name appears beside his parents' on the [[homestead-deed-1905]] for the Cedar Grove claim. He farmed the same one hundred sixty acres until his death, serving a decade as the town's postmaster on the side.
