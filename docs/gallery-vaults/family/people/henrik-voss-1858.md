Henrik Voss worked the Riga docks as a shipwright before marrying [[magdalena-voss-1861]] per the [[marriage-banns-1880]] in 1880. Family lore insists he was widowed once already, though nothing confirms it beyond a faint entry some suspect belongs to the [[parish-register-1871]]. That silence is the whole of [[who-was-henriks-first-wife]]. He later broke prairie sod in Cedar Grove, Minnesota, dying a farmer rather than a dockhand.
