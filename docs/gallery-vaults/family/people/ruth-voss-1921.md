Ruth Voss, daughter of the blacksmith [[oskar-voss-1890]], taught school in Cedar Grove for over thirty years and kept every scrap of paper her father left behind. Stories about her great-grandmother [[magdalena-voss-1861]] crossing the Baltic never left her, and she was first to flag the confusion on the [[farm-auction-notice-1931]]. It was Ruth who named the whole snarl [[two-oskars-problem]] after finding a second Oskar Voss entirely.
