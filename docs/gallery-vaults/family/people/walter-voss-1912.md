Walter Voss, son of [[arvid-voss-1884]] and [[ilse-lindqvist-1893]], grew up working the same fields his grandmother [[magdalena-voss-1861]] had crossed an ocean to reach. He took over the homestead outright after his father's death and married in a ceremony captured in the [[wedding-photo-1936]]. Neighbors remembered him for rotating crops before it was fashionable to do so.
