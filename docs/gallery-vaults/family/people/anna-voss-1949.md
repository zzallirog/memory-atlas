Anna Voss, younger child of [[walter-voss-1912]] and sister to [[harold-voss-1946]], became the family's unofficial archivist, tracking cousins scattered across the Midwest. She traced her fascination with the past straight back to the [[wedding-photo-1936]] hanging in her parents' hall. It was Anna who first started asking hard questions about the family's Baltic side.
