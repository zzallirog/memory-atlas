Greta Voss, second daughter of [[magdalena-voss-1861]], took up sewing work in Cedar Grove and kept a long correspondence gathered later as the [[letters-from-greta-1907-1919]]. County ledgers name [[karl-lindqvist-1889]] as her husband, though whether the marriage ever properly held is the crux of [[gretas-missing-husband]]. Her own letters mention a husband only in passing, never quite by name.
