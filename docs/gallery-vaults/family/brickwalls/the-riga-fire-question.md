Some branches of the family insist a fire tore through the Riga district where [[magdalena-voss-1861]] and [[henrik-voss-1858]] once lived, destroying whatever records might have named Henrik's first wife. No newspaper account of such a fire has been located, and the only supposed proof is a scorched page rumored to belong to the [[parish-register-1871]]. It may be true history, or simply a story invented to explain a gap nobody could otherwise fill.
