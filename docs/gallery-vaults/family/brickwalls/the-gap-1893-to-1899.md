[[ilse-lindqvist-1893]] and her brother [[karl-lindqvist-1889]] appear nowhere in any book, letter, or ledger the family holds until the [[census-sheet-1910]] catches them already married into the Voss household. Six years, maybe more, sit empty between whatever brought them to Cedar Grove and that census page. No shipping record, no parish entry, nothing has ever closed the gap.
