[[oskar-voss-1890]]'s name turns up on documents that cannot both be true of one man, including a naturalization filed the same week as a land dispute two counties away. His daughter [[ruth-voss-1921]] was first to notice the overlap, tracing it back through the [[naturalization-petition-1913]]. Whether Cedar Grove hosted two unrelated Oskar Vosses or one very busy one has never been settled.
