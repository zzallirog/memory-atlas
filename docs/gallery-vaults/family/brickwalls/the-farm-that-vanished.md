The [[farm-auction-notice-1931]] announces the sale of [[sigrid-voss-1924]]'s childhood home, but no resale deed, tax record, or later owner has ever been traced. The last map to show its boundaries clearly is the missing [[county-plat-map-1928]], referenced in three other documents but held by no archive anyone has found. The acreage itself seems to have simply stopped existing on paper.
