[[dora-voss-1915]] appears smiling in the [[wedding-photo-1936]] and then nowhere else, not in a marriage index, obituary, or later census anywhere in the state. Cousins have guessed Minneapolis, guessed worse, and guessed nothing at all with equal confidence. Not one letter or postcard from her has ever turned up after 1940.
