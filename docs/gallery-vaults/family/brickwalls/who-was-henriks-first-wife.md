description: Open question - was Henrik Voss really widowed before marrying Magdalena in 1880?
tags: [open]
---
The clerk who recorded the 1880 banns for [[henrik-voss-1858]] and [[magdalena-voss-1861]] described Henrik as a widower, but no first marriage has ever surfaced in any surviving book. The best lead is a scorched fragment thought to belong to the [[parish-register-1871]], which may or may not even be the right parish. Until that fragment turns up whole, the first wife has no name at all.
