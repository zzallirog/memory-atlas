tags: [open]

Nothing in [[magdalena-voss-1861]]'s own words explains why the family left Riga in 1903, and the [[steamship-manifest-1903]] only records that they did, not why. Unrest along the Baltic coast that year is the leading guess, backed loosely by the discrepancies in the [[ellis-island-manifest-1903]]. Economic hardship is the other theory, and the family has never agreed on which one to believe.
