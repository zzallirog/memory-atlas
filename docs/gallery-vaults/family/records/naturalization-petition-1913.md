Three men from the same Cedar Grove township filed together in 1913: [[henrik-voss-1858]], [[karl-lindqvist-1889]], and [[oskar-voss-1890]]. All three list the same witness and the same courthouse date, suggesting they traveled together to file. Karl's signature is notably shakier than the other two, likely from a carpentry injury that year.
