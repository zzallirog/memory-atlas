description: 1943 wartime ration book shared by the Walter Voss household in Cedar Grove.
tags: [civil]
---
Issued in 1943, the ration book lists [[walter-voss-1912]] as household head, with his sister [[dora-voss-1915]] and cousin [[ernst-lindqvist-1918]] sharing coupons that lean year. Sugar and gasoline stamps are torn out entirely; the meat stamps are untouched. It is one of the few wartime records to survive the family's periodic attic clear-outs.
