The same Riga parish that married [[magdalena-voss-1861]] and [[henrik-voss-1858]] recorded the baptism of their first son, [[arvid-voss-1884]], later that decade. The entry lists Henrik's occupation as shipwright, one of the few surviving clues to his working life before emigration. Ink has faded badly along the book's spine, but the names are still legible.
