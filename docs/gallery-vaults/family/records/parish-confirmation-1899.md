The register records [[arvid-voss-1884]] confirmed at fifteen alongside his younger sister [[greta-voss-1887]], confirmed early at twelve, with their father [[henrik-voss-1858]] signing as witness. It is the last parish document naming the family before their 1903 emigration. The page is water-stained along its bottom edge, obscuring the presiding pastor's name.
