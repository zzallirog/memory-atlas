The 1910 census catches [[arvid-voss-1884]] and his young wife [[ilse-lindqvist-1893]] sharing a Cedar Grove household with his bachelor brother [[oskar-voss-1890]]. It is the first record where Ilse's married name and Arvid's appear side by side. The enumerator misspelled the family name twice on the same page.
