Across a dozen years, [[greta-voss-1887]] wrote home to [[magdalena-voss-1861]] about sewing work, church socials, and a husband she never quite named. The bundle is thinner than family memory suggests, and cousins have long argued a companion volume, the so-called [[gretas-diary]], once existed and said far more plainly. Most letters are postmarked from Cedar Grove, a few curiously from a town two counties over.
