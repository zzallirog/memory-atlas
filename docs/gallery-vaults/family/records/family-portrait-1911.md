A traveling photographer captured [[magdalena-voss-1861]] seated stiffly beside [[arvid-voss-1884]], [[greta-voss-1887]], and [[oskar-voss-1890]] outside the Cedar Grove general store in 1911. It is the only known photograph showing all three of her surviving children as adults in one frame. The original print has a long crease straight through Oskar's shoulder.
