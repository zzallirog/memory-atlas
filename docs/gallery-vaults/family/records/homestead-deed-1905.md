description: 1905 Minnesota homestead deed naming Magdalena, Henrik, and son Arvid Voss.
tags: [civil]
---
Two years after landing, [[magdalena-voss-1861]] and [[henrik-voss-1858]] filed for one hundred sixty acres of Cedar Grove prairie under the Homestead Act. Their son [[arvid-voss-1884]] is listed as a witness, already twenty-one and working the same soil his parents claimed. The deed's county stamp is the earliest solid proof of exactly where the family settled.
