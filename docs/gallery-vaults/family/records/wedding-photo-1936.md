The photograph shows [[walter-voss-1912]] on his wedding day in 1936, his sister [[dora-voss-1915]] standing just behind the bride, and a seventy-five-year-old [[magdalena-voss-1861]] seated in front in her best dark dress. It remains the only image showing three generations of the family together. Dora is smiling in it in a way no later photograph ever quite matches.
