---
name: steamship-manifest-1903
description: 1903 passenger manifest listing the Voss family's crossing to the United States.
tags: [ship]
---
The manifest lists [[magdalena-voss-1861]], [[henrik-voss-1858]], and a thirteen-year-old [[oskar-voss-1890]] among the steerage passengers bound for New York in 1903. A second, contested copy known as the [[ellis-island-manifest-1903]] repeats Oskar's name in a different hand, which is where the family's identity confusion first took root. The ship's name has been read three different ways across three photocopies.
