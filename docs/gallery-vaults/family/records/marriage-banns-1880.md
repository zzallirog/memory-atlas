The banns were read three Sundays running in a Riga parish in 1880, announcing the marriage of [[magdalena-voss-1861]] to [[henrik-voss-1858]]. The clerk's hand noted Henrik as a widower, a detail that sent later researchers hunting through the ghost of a [[parish-register-1871]] for a name that was never found. The banns themselves survive only as a photocopy passed down through the family.
