tags: [civil]

The notice announces the forced sale of [[oskar-voss-1890]]'s farm in 1931, acreage referenced on the old [[county-plat-map-1928]] that has since gone missing from the courthouse files. Neighbors always called it the start of [[the-farm-that-vanished]], since no deed of resale was ever recorded. The printed notice survives only because a neighbor clipped it for her scrapbook.
