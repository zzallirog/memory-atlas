Kept the books for Hale's Landing marina from 1994 onward and prepared the
[[marina-ledger-1998]] entries later scrutinized by the state auditor. In
September 1998 he privately raised concerns about a signature on a loan
document, concerns that fed directly into the [[forged-signature-analysis]]
commissioned that winter. His formal statement, taken 2 November 1998, is
preserved in [[okafor-interview-notes]].
