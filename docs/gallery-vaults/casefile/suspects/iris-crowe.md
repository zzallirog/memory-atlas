Married to [[dennis-crowe]] since 1988, she told detectives on 15 October that
her husband arrived home by 10 PM, a detail no one had asked for. A message
she left on Marta Hale's [[answering-machine-tape]] earlier that evening asked
about a delayed fuel delivery. Her account of the evening became the spine of
[[crowe-alibi-timeline]], compiled by the sheriff's office in November 1998.
