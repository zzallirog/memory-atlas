Marta's only son, estranged from the family since a 1995 falling-out and
living out of state under an address never confirmed by investigators. He
was named in [[the-revised-will]] for the first time since the estrangement,
restored to a minor bequest at [[gordon-hale]]'s reported urging. His only
contact with the family afterward survives in [[tobias-postcards]], sent
without a consistent return address.
