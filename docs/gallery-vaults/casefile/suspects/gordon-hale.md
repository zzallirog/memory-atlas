Marta's older brother, removed from her 1989 will after a dispute over their
late mother's lakehouse and reinstated as a beneficiary in the version
drafted in 1998. [[counsel-arden]] handled the redraft and later told
investigators Gordon had visited the office twice that summer. His daughter
[[petra-hale-voss]] said he seemed unusually anxious about
[[the-revised-will]]'s final terms in the weeks before Marta's death.
