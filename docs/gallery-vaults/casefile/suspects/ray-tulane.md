Worked as a seasonal dockhand at Hale's Landing marina until Marta Hale let
him go on 6 September 1998 over a pay dispute. He appears at the edge of one
frame in the [[dock-camera-polaroids]], though the timestamp is illegible. He
told deputies [[the-second-witness]] saw him at the Route 9 Motel bar that
night, a claim recorded in the [[motel-registry-page]] margin but never
corroborated.
