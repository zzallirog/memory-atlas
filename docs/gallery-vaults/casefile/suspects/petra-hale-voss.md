---
Named executor under [[the-revised-will]] signed 2 September 1998, a role she
had not held under the prior 1989 version. Daughter of [[gordon-hale]], she
filed the initial probate paperwork on 3 November 1998, three weeks after
Marta's death. Her early filings are referenced throughout
[[probate-fight-notes]], which document objections raised by other family
members.
