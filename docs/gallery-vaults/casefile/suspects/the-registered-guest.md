name: the-registered-guest
description: Motel guest who checked in under a false name the week Marta Hale died.
tags: [theory-drifter]
---
Checked into the Route 9 Motel on 11 October 1998 under the name "J. Vance,"
an entry preserved in the [[motel-registry-page]] the clerk later flagged. A
guest matching his description appears at the marina dock's edge in one of
the [[dock-camera-polaroids]] taken 12 October. Investigators could not match
his fingerprints to the [[unmatched-print-donor]] profile lifted from the
scene.
