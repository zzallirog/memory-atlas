Cedar Falls attorney who drafted [[the-revised-will]] over three sessions in
August and September 1998, billing records for which appear in
[[arden-billing-hours]]. Represented the Hale family for over a decade and
had previously handled a dispute involving [[tobias-hale]]'s trust
distribution in 1994. Declined to discuss the will's contents with
investigators beyond confirming its execution date.
