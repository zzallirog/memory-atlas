County jail intake log listing four vagrancy arrests made along Route 9
during October 1998, none naming [[ray-tulane]] directly. One entry, dated
16 October, matches a physical description close to [[the-registered-guest]]
but lists no fixed address. A pawned watch mentioned in the intake notes was
traced to a [[pawnshop-ledger]] page that has since gone missing from the
county archive.
