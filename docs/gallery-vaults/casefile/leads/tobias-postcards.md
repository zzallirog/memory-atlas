Three postcards sent by [[tobias-hale]] to a cousin between October and
December 1998, postmarked from towns along the state's western border. None
mention Marta's death directly, though one from November references "the
lawyer's letter," presumed to concern [[the-revised-will]]. The postmarks
were later compared against unrelated regional travel logs without a clear
match.
