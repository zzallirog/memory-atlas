Billing ledger from [[counsel-arden]]'s firm itemizing hours charged to the
Hale estate between July and October 1998, including three sessions tied to
[[the-revised-will]]. A gap in billed hours between 14 and 20 October drew
scrutiny during a later review. The ledger was entered as an exhibit in
[[probate-fight-notes]] after opposing counsel questioned its completeness.
