Reconstruction of the storm front that crossed Cedar Falls between 12 and
13 October 1998, drawn from National Weather Service records requested in
2001. Heavy rain from roughly 9 PM to 1 AM would have limited visibility
along the road past the [[motel-registry-page]] location. The window
overlaps entries compiled independently in [[route-9-sightings]].
