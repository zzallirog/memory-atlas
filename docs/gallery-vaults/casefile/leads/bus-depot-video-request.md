Formal request filed 21 October 1998 for security footage from the Cedar
Falls Greyhound depot, sent after the [[greyhound-ticket-stub]] surfaced in
the storm drain. The bus operator's regional office replied that footage was
recorded over on a 72-hour cycle and none survived from the relevant dates.
The request had been prompted by a deputy comparing depot hours against the
[[motel-registry-page]] check-in and the broader [[route-9-sightings]] log.
