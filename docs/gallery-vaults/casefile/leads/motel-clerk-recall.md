Recorded interview from March 2004 in which the Route 9 Motel's former night
clerk recalled the [[motel-registry-page]] entry for room 6, though by then
admitting uncertainty about the date. She described [[the-registered-guest]]
as unusually insistent on paying cash. She also mentioned
[[the-second-witness]], allegedly seated in the parking lot that night, a
person she could no longer name.
