Compiled by the Cedar Falls sheriff's office in November 1998 to reconstruct
[[dennis-crowe]]'s movements between 9:00 PM and midnight on 13 October.
Cross-referenced against the final entries in [[marina-ledger-1998]], the
timeline left a 25-minute gap unaccounted for near the marina office. A
clerical note in the margin references the [[motel-registry-page]] from the
Route 9 motel, though no connection to Crowe was ever established.
