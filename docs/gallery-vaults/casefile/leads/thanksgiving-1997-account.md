Compiled recollections of the Hale family's Thanksgiving gathering on
27 November 1997, the last the family held together before Marta's death.
[[gordon-hale]] and [[petra-hale-voss]] gave differing accounts of a heated
exchange over the lakehouse that evening. A cousin's account referenced
[[the-second-witness]], present at the table, whose identity was never
recorded in any other file.
