Transcript of [[len-okafor]]'s statement to the Cedar Falls sheriff's office,
taken 2 November 1998, in which he described the loan-modification signature
that prompted the [[forged-signature-analysis]]. He also walked investigators
through three months of entries in [[marina-ledger-1998]], pointing to
deposits he could not account for. The interview ran ninety minutes and was
never followed by a second session.
