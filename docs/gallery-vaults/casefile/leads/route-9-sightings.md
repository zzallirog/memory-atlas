Log compiled by a sheriff's deputy of reported sightings along Route 9 for
the week of 11 to 17 October 1998, including the discovery site of the
[[greyhound-ticket-stub]]. Weather notes in the log overlap with those in
[[the-october-storm-window]], suggesting visibility was poor after dusk. A
stray cross-reference to [[tobias-postcards]] was added in 1999 after a
postmark from a nearby town surfaced in an unrelated file.
