Marta Hale's will, re-executed 2 September 1998, restored her estranged
brother and added her son as a beneficiary for the first time since 1995. The
revision references a [[safe-deposit-inventory]] filed that same week and
reassigns the family's [[lakehouse-key-count]] property. Its terms were
contested within days of probate opening, a dispute chronicled in
[[probate-fight-notes]].
