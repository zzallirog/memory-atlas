Microcassette pulled from Marta Hale's kitchen answering machine on
15 October 1998, its final message timestamped 8:52 PM the night before she
was found. The recorded caller identified herself as [[iris-crowe]], asking
about a fuel delivery attributed to [[dennis-crowe]]. Investigators noted the
call time fell inside a gap in the [[marina-ledger-1998]] deposit log.
