Handwritten ledger covering Hale's Landing marina's income and expenses for
1998, maintained in [[len-okafor]]'s hand through September and in Marta's
afterward. Entries stop on 13 October, the day of a $4,200 slip-rental deposit
that was never reconciled, a gap later itemized in [[marina-debt-workup]]. A
torn page near the back lists a hardware order that investigators matched to
the [[boathouse-padlock]] replaced that same month.
