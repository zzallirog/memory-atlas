Single page from the Route 9 Motel's guest register covering 11 through
13 October 1998, room 6 assigned to [[the-registered-guest]]. The night
clerk's recollection of the entry, given years later, is recorded in
[[motel-clerk-recall]]. The page was cross-filed with [[route-9-sightings]]
after a deputy noted the motel sat four miles from the marina turnoff.
