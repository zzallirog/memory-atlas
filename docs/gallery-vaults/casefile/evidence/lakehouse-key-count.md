Reconciliation of keys to the Hale family lakehouse, prepared for probate in
December 1998 against terms set out in [[the-revised-will]]. Of five keys
originally cut, only three were located, cross-checked against the
[[safe-deposit-inventory]] taken the prior month. [[gordon-hale]] told the
estate attorney he had returned his copy in 1996, a claim the inventory could
not confirm.
