Inventory of the contents of Marta Hale's safe deposit box at Cedar Falls
Trust, opened under court order on 12 November 1998. Contents included a
notarized copy of [[the-revised-will]] and a set of spare keys later compared
against the [[lakehouse-key-count]]. No cash or jewelry beyond a modest coin
collection was recorded.
