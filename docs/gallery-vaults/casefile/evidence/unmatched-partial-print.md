Latent partial print lifted from the dock railing at Hale's Landing on
15 October 1998, attributed only to the [[unmatched-print-donor]]
designation. Its position on the railing corresponds to a figure visible in
one of the [[dock-camera-polaroids]] taken three days earlier. Enlargements
were later compared against [[coroner-photos-roll-2]], a roll the lab log
describes as water-damaged.
