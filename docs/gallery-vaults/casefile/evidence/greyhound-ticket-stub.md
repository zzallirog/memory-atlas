Torn bus ticket stub, Cedar Falls to Millbrook, found in a storm drain near
the Route 9 Motel on 18 October 1998. The purchase date, 10 October, places
it two days before [[the-registered-guest]] checked into room 6. A pawn
receipt folded inside the stub referenced a [[pawnshop-ledger]] transaction
that was never located, and the stub itself surfaces again in
[[route-9-sightings]].
