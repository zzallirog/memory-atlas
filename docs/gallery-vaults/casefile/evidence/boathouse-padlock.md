A new brass padlock on the Hale's Landing boathouse, installed 4 October 1998
to replace one [[dennis-crowe]] reported as lost. The hardware purchase
appears as an unexplained $38 line in [[marina-ledger-1998]], filed under
general repairs. No spare key was ever recovered from Marta Hale's effects.
