A photocopy of Marta Hale's $180,000 key-person life insurance policy,
underwritten by Hartford Mutual in March 1996, recovered from a marina filing
cabinet on 20 October 1998. Marta and Dennis Crowe named each other as sole
beneficiaries under the buyout rider, a term [[len-okafor]] had drafted into
the [[marina-ledger-1998]] appendix. The policy became the basis of
[[the-hartford-claim]] filed nine days after her death.
