Series of instant photographs from the marina's fixed dock camera, covering
12 and 13 October 1998, several frames overexposed by afternoon glare. One
frame shows a figure resembling [[the-registered-guest]] near the fuel pump
at 4:15 PM. The camera's timestamp was later cross-checked against the
[[motel-registry-page]] check-in time for consistency.
