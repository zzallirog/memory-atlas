Itemized phone bill for the kitchen line at Marta Hale's house, covering
September and October 1998, subpoenaed by the estate's probate attorney in
1999. Three calls to an out-of-state number were later matched to
[[tobias-hale]], the last placed six days before [[the-revised-will]] was
executed. A call logged the evening of 13 October corresponds to the message
preserved on the [[answering-machine-tape]].
